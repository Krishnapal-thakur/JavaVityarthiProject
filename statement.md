
Problem Statement
University libraries manage complex inventories composed of thousands of physical assets while simultaneously serving diverse user roles (Students, Faculty, Staff) with varying borrowing privileges. Relying on manual record-keeping or decentralized legacy systems results in significant critical operational bottlenecks. Manual tracking causes inventory discrepancies, misplaced volumes, and persistent data vulnerabilities because persistent state is not rigorously tracked. Additionally, manually enforcing role-based privileges—such as different book limits and loan periods—and calculating late fines for hundreds of users is inefficient, prone to human error, and leads to uncollected fees and delayed asset circulation.

The Smart University Library & Resource Management System (ULRMS) addresses these challenges by digitalizing the complete circulation lifecycle, automated privilege enforcement, multithreaded fine accruals, and incorporating robust exception handling.

Scope of the Project
